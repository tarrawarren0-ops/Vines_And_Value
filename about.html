Our story & purpose

Helping communities find healthy food and grow stronger together.

Everyone should be able to get healthy food, no matter where they live or how much money they have.

Who we are

Getting food can be about more than what is in the store.

Vines & Values is a nonprofit project idea. We want to help people across New Jersey find healthy food they can afford. Every family should be able to choose food that works for them and their budget.

We focus on places where fresh groceries may be hard to find. High prices, long trips to stores, few choices, and busy schedules can all make grocery shopping harder.

Why this work matters

Local problems need local solutions.

Camden, Paterson, and Newark are some of the New Jersey communities affected by food access challenges. People should not have to face these problems alone, especially when stores are far away or choices are limited.

We share information and support local ideas. We do not judge the choices people make. Our resources should be clear, useful, and respectful of different needs and budgets.
