Our story & purpose

Growing opportunities for healthier, more connected communities.

We believe access to healthy food should not depend on someone's neighborhood or financial situation.

Who we are

Food access is about more than food.

Vines & Values is a nonprofit organization concept focused on addressing food insecurity and improving access to affordable, nutritious food throughout New Jersey. We believe every family deserves the opportunity to find food that meets their needs and fits their budget.

Our project is especially interested in communities where residents have fewer convenient options for purchasing fresh groceries. Prices, transportation, store selection, and the time people have available can all influence where they shop and what they are able to bring home.

Why this work matters

Local challenges deserve thoughtful, local solutions.

New Jersey communities such as Camden, Paterson, and Newark are important to conversations about food access. Residents should not be expected to solve every barrier on their own, especially when transportation or nearby food choices are limited.

Vines & Values aims to share information and encourage local solutions rather than judge the choices people make. We want our resources to be respectful, understandable, and practical for people with different schedules, budgets, and needs.
