Our story & purpose

Helping communities find healthy food and grow stronger together.

Everyone deserves access to nutritious food, regardless of where they live or what they can afford.

Who we are

Food access involves more than the availability of grocery stores.

Vines & Value is a nonprofit project concept focused on improving access to affordable, nutritious food across New Jersey. We believe individuals and families should have the information and resources they need to make food choices that fit their needs and budgets.

Our focus includes communities where access to fresh groceries may be limited. Food costs, transportation barriers, limited store options, and busy schedules can all affect a household’s ability to shop for groceries.

Why this work matters

Local problems need local solutions.

Communities such as Camden, Paterson, and Newark continue to experience challenges with food access. Distance to grocery stores, transportation, and limited food options can affect the choices available to residents.

We share practical information and highlight local efforts that support food access. Our goal is to provide clear, useful resources that respect each person’s circumstances, preferences, and budget.
