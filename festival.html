A gathering with a purpose

Making healthy food easier to choose.

Fresh for Less is a planned community food festival idea from Vines & Values. It would bring neighbors and local groups together to share ways to find healthy food at lower prices.

The festival would be a friendly place to learn and connect. Visitors could get tips on low-cost foods, meal planning, and wasting less food. They could also meet people working to make food easier to get.

Community voices matter.

People who live in a neighborhood know what it needs and what makes it special. Their voices should be part of talks about food access. Fresh for Less would bring people together to share ideas and useful resources.
