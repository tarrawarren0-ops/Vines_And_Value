A gathering with a purpose

Making healthy food easier to choose.

Fresh for Less is a proposed community food festival from Vines & Values. The event would bring residents and local organizations together to share resources and practical ways to access nutritious food at lower costs.

The festival would offer opportunities to learn, connect, and explore local resources. Visitors could learn about affordable food options, meal planning, and reducing food waste while connecting with organizations working to improve food access.

Learn from local experts

Guest speakers and take-home resources.

The festival is planned to feature three professional speakers:

01

Food insecurity expert

Sharing information about food insecurity and barriers to reliable food access.

02

Registered dietitian or nutrition professional

Offering guidance on making nutritious food choices while staying within a budget.

03

Food waste expert

Discussing ways to reduce food waste and make better use of available food.

Speaker names and final program details will be shared when they are confirmed. Attendees are also expected to receive Vines & Values educational booklets with affordable recipes, grocery information, and practical tips.

Rooted in local voices

Community voices matter.

Residents bring important knowledge about the needs and strengths of their communities. Their perspectives should help shape conversations and solutions around food access. Fresh for Less would create space for neighbors and local organizations to share ideas and resources.
