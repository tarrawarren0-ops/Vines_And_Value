Fresh for Less

A community food festival concept rooted in connection, learning, and fresh possibilities.

A gathering with a purpose

Fresh for Less is a proposed community food festival created as part of the Vines & Values project. The goal is to bring residents and community organizations together to talk about affordable food, local resources, and practical ways to make nutritious choices.

The event concept is designed to feel welcoming and useful. Visitors could learn about affordable ingredients, meal planning, and ways to reduce food waste, while hearing from people working to improve local food access.

What visitors could explore

  • Information about affordable and nutritious food options.
  • Ideas for planning meals and making groceries go further.
  • Tips for storing food and reducing household waste.
  • Conversations about transportation and neighborhood food access.
  • Opportunities to connect with local organizations and neighbors.

Community voices matter

Residents understand the needs and strengths of their neighborhoods. Their experiences should be part of conversations about improving food access. Fresh for Less is intended to create space for those conversations while sharing resources in a clear and approachable way.
