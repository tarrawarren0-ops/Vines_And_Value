Fresh for Less.

A day of food, learning, and community—bringing people together to explore ways to make fresh, nutritious food more affordable.

Discover the festival
A community food festival

Good food should be within reach.

Fresh for Less is a Vines & Value community festival focused on food access, affordable nutrition, and stronger local connections. The event will bring Camden residents together with community organizations and food experts.

Guests can learn practical ways to shop on a budget, reduce food waste, and find local food resources. The day is designed to share useful information people can take home and put to work in their everyday lives.

Ideas from people with experience

Learn from food and community experts.

The festival program is planned to include three guest speakers who will share information, answer questions, and offer practical guidance.

01

Food access

A food insecurity expert will discuss why access to reliable, affordable food matters and what communities can do to help.

02

Nutrition on a budget

A dietitian or nutrition professional will share ways to make nutritious choices while keeping costs in mind.

03

Less food waste

A food waste expert will offer ideas for using food well, storing it safely, and reducing waste.

Speaker names and the final program will be shared when confirmed.

Helpful information to take home

Keep the ideas going after the festival.

Guests will be able to take home Vines & Value educational booklets with affordable recipes, information about nearby grocery stores, and useful tips for shopping and eating well on a budget.

Local markets are also planned as part of the festival, creating opportunities to explore produce options and support neighborhood businesses.

Building a stronger food community

Fresh ideas. Local impact.

Fresh for Less is part of a larger effort to make affordable, nutritious food easier to find in communities that need more options. By bringing residents, local businesses, and community partners together, we can share ideas and help build a stronger local food system.
