Fresh for Less Community Food Festival

Sharing ideas, building connections, and celebrating community.

🌱 About the Festival

Fresh for Less is a proposed community food festival created as part of the Vines & Values project. The goal is to bring residents and community organizations together to talk about affordable food, local resources, and practical ways to make nutritious choices. Rather than treating food insecurity as an issue that individuals must solve alone, the festival is designed around sharing information and building connections.

The event concept focuses on making the experience welcoming and useful. Visitors could learn about affordable ingredients, ways to plan meals, and methods for reducing food waste at home. The festival would also provide an opportunity to hear from speakers and community participants who can discuss local food access challenges and possible responses.

What Visitors Can Explore

  • Information about affordable and nutritious food options.
  • Ideas for planning meals and making groceries go further.
  • Tips for storing food and reducing household waste.
  • Conversations about transportation and neighborhood food access.
  • Opportunities to connect with local organizations and community members.

💚 Why Community Participation Matters

Residents understand the needs and strengths of their neighborhoods. Their experiences should be part of conversations about improving food access. Fresh for Less is intended to create space for those conversations while sharing resources in a clear and approachable way. The festival is one part of a larger vision: supporting communities as they work toward food options that are affordable, accessible, and sustainable.
