Benefits basics

Understanding SNAP / EBT

A little information can make grocery benefits easier to navigate.

01

What is SNAP?

The Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) helps eligible households pay for groceries. In New Jersey, benefits are placed on a Families First EBT card, which works similarly to a debit card at participating retailers.

Learn about NJ SNAP ↗ · Using benefits ↗
02

Can SNAP buy fresh produce?

Yes. SNAP can be used for eligible foods such as fresh fruits and vegetables, bread, cereal, dairy, meat, and seeds or plants that produce food. It cannot be used for non-food household items or hot prepared foods.

A fresh perspective

Affordable produce & less food waste

Some fruits and vegetables are overlooked because they have unusual shapes, minor cosmetic marks, or come from surplus harvests. This produce can still be nutritious and delicious.

Markets that sell or rescue surplus and imperfect produce can help keep edible food in circulation instead of letting it go to waste. Misfits Market is one example of a grocery business that sources food based on quality and flavor rather than appearance. Pricing and delivery options vary, so shoppers should compare costs and check availability.
