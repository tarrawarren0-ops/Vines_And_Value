Grocery transportation

According to The Philadelphia Inquirer, New Beginnings Behavioral Health operates a free grocery transportation service for Camden residents. Supported by a FEED NJ grant, the service provides rides to ShopRite and other grocery stores in Cherry Hill.

Ask about a ride 856-588-0807

Contact New Beginnings Behavioral Health to confirm current availability, pick-up locations, and eligibility. The article reports that rides operate on weekdays and some Saturdays.

Benefits basics

Understanding SNAP / EBT

Learn more about how SNAP benefits work and which foods can be purchased with an EBT card.

01

What is SNAP?

The Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) helps eligible households pay for groceries. In New Jersey, benefits are added to a Families First EBT card, which can be used like a debit card at participating retailers.

Learn about NJ SNAP ↗ · Using benefits ↗
02

Can SNAP buy fresh produce?

Yes. SNAP benefits can be used to purchase eligible foods, including fresh fruits and vegetables, bread, cereal, milk, meat, and seeds or plants that produce food. Benefits cannot be used for household items or most hot prepared foods.

A fresh perspective

Affordable produce & less food waste

Produce may vary in size, shape, or appearance and still be fresh and nutritious. Some retailers also offer surplus produce that might otherwise go unused.

Some retailers sell surplus or imperfect-looking produce to help reduce food waste. Misfits Market is one example of a company that offers these products. Availability, pricing, and delivery options may vary, so compare costs and review current offerings before ordering.
