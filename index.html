Everyone deserves access to fresh, nutritious, and affordable food. Vines & Values is focused on helping New Jersey communities find practical ways to improve food access and build healthier futures.

🌱 Building a Healthier New Jersey

Food insecurity affects families and communities in different ways. For some residents, finding affordable fresh food can involve more than choosing what to buy. The location of grocery stores, transportation options, and the prices of available foods can all affect everyday decisions.

Vines & Values wants to support practical community-centered approaches. Our focus includes sharing useful information, encouraging access to affordable produce, and promoting ways to reduce food waste.

🍃 Our Mission

Our mission is to make healthy, affordable food more accessible while supporting stronger and more sustainable communities. We believe local ideas and community participation can be part of creating meaningful change.
