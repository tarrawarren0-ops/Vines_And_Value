Why it matters

Healthy food should be part of every neighborhood.

Food insecurity affects families and communities in different ways. For some residents, finding affordable fresh food involves more than choosing what to buy. Grocery store locations, transportation, food prices, and the selection available nearby all shape everyday decisions.
