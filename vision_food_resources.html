Food Resources in Camden, NJ

Connecting our community with food assistance, grocery access, and affordable produce.

Finding food should be easier.

Food access can involve more than the cost of groceries. Transportation, store locations, and knowing where to find assistance can all make a difference. This page shares local food programs and practical information for Camden residents.

Resource reminder: Program schedules and eligibility requirements can change. Please contact the organization or check its website before visiting.

🚌 Grocery Transportation

Free rides to grocery stores in Cherry Hill

A Philadelphia Inquirer report describes a free door-to-door grocery shuttle for Camden residents operated through New Beginnings Behavioral Health and supported by a FEED NJ grant. The article reports service to ShopRite and other Cherry Hill stores.

To ask about a ride: Call New Beginnings Behavioral Health at 856-588-0807. The article reports that service runs Monday through Friday, with some Saturdays. Call to confirm current availability, pickup details, and eligibility before making plans.

💳 SNAP / EBT Basics

What is SNAP?

The Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) helps eligible households pay for groceries. In New Jersey, benefits are placed on a Families First EBT card, which works similarly to a debit card at participating retailers.

Learn about NJ SNAP · How to use benefits

Can SNAP buy fresh produce?

Yes. SNAP can be used to purchase eligible foods such as fresh fruits and vegetables, as well as bread, cereal, dairy, meat, and seeds or plants that produce food. SNAP cannot be used for non-food household items or hot prepared foods.

🌱 Affordable Produce & Less Food Waste

Imperfect produce can still be good food.

Some fruits and vegetables are overlooked because they have unusual shapes, minor cosmetic marks, or come from surplus harvests. Markets that sell or rescue this produce can help keep edible food in circulation instead of letting it go to waste.

Misfits Market is one example of a grocery business that sources food based on quality and flavor rather than appearance. Its pricing and delivery options vary, so shoppers should compare costs and check availability. This approach also connects to Vines & Value's idea of making nutritious food more affordable through community markets.
